Document preparation

What to assemble before the first filing

  • Passport validity and proposed travel dates
  • Birth, marriage, divorce, and child documents from the correct issuing authority
  • Police certificates and the correct authentication or apostille sequence
  • Pension, rental, investment, business, or employment income evidence
  • Spanish translations and retained copies of originals that authorities may keep
  • A route-specific budget separating government and professional charges
Dominican immigration status and Canadian tax residence are separate questions. Extended stays, departure ties, pensions, property, and investment income should be reviewed with qualified Canadian and Dominican tax advisers.
