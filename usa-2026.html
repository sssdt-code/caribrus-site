Your case map

What CaribRus reviews before recommending a route

  • Citizenship and current state/country of residence
  • Income source: employment, pension, rent, business, or investment
  • Spouse and children, including countries where civil documents were issued
  • Property plans and whether the contemplated investment fits the selected category
  • Travel date, passport validity, police certificates, apostilles, and translations
  • Long-term objective: relocation, backup residency, permanent status, or naturalization
Dominican residency does not by itself end US tax filing or reporting obligations. Tax advice must come from a qualified adviser who knows the client's complete facts.
