Confirmed official updateJCE · Published August 27, 2026

Cédula renewal is open regardless of birth month.

JCE removed the staged birth-month restriction from August 28 and announced the end date for acceptance of the previous cédula format.

What is confirmed: from August 28, 2026, Dominican citizens may attend JCE service centers to renew the new cédula regardless of their month of birth.

Deadline: JCE says the previous cédula format will stop being accepted on April 1, 2027. Last verified by CaribRus September 14, 2026.

Practical consequence

Eligible citizens no longer need to wait for the month assigned under the earlier staged rollout. Anyone still holding the previous format should plan renewal before the announced deadline rather than waiting until the final weeks.

Important scope limit

The announcement concerns renewal of the cédula. It does not state that first issuance after naturalization, civil-registry transcription, correction of surnames, or anotación complementaria follows the same simplified path.

Recommended preparation

  • Confirm the appropriate JCE service center and any appointment requirement.
  • Separate an ordinary renewal from first issuance or a civil-record correction.
  • Keep copies of supporting civil-status documents when names or surnames require review.

This update summarizes the cited JCE announcement and does not replace case-specific instructions from JCE.

Official source

Junta Central Electoral (JCE), official announcement published August 27, 2026.
